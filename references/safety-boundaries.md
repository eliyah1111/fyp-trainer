# Safety Boundaries

FYP Trainer is a transparent user-assistive workflow, not a stealth automation kit.

## Allowed

- Opening TikTok in a visible browser controlled by the user.
- Waiting for the user to log in manually.
- Generating a taste profile and asking for `Y/N` approval.
- Searching, opening videos, scrolling, and watching briefly.
- Classifying visible video text, captions, hashtags, sounds, URLs, and surrounding text.
- Caching local search quality signals to avoid repeating weak searches.
- Applying tiny capped preference signals after confirmation.
- Refreshing the feed at the end.

## Not Allowed

- Collecting or storing TikTok credentials.
- Bypassing CAPTCHA, age gates, safety prompts, or login protections.
- Using stealth flags, fingerprint spoofing, or anti-detection tooling.
- Mass liking, following, commenting, messaging, posting, uploading, scraping, or evading platform limits.
- Hundreds of robotic live searches, views, or Not Interested actions per minute.
- Running live account-changing actions before confirmation.
